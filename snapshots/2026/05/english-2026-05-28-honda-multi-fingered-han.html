FEATURE: Honda revives robotics ambitions after ASIMO

By Kie Koshika,KYODO NEWS
- Aug 06, 2026 - 08:42 All,Japan,Feature

TOKYO - Honda Motor Co. has developed a robotic hand capable of highly dexterous movements, marking a return to robotics with a new industrial focus after retiring its once-iconic ASIMO humanoid robot.

The company plans to commercialize the multi-fingered hand in the early 2030s for use in manufacturing, aiming to help automate factory work as labor shortages intensify.
