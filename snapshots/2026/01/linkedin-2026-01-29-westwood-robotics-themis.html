Westwood Robotics is proud to announce a major update: THEMIS Gen2.5, the world’s first commercial full-size humanoid robot capable of manipulation on the move! 🚀 Powered by AOS: With this release, we’re officially rolling out AOS (AI-Augmented Humanoid OS), built around three core modules: - Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation Controller with sensor-fusion-based state estimation for accurate, stable motion control; - Navigation Module with multi-layer mapping, semantic understanding, and path planning; - Interaction Module, powered by OC-VAM (Object-Centric Vision-Action Model) for compute-efficient task planning. 🦾 Hardware Upgrades: THEMIS has also been tremendously improved in hardware: - Redesigned structure for 40% greater impact resistance - Upgraded arms to 7-DoF, with over 5 kg peak payload per arm - New Mountain BEAR actuators in the hip joints, delivering over 120% torque capacity and ~80% less heat generation The future of automation keeps moving! * This video is for technical demonstration purposes only. Standing 1.73 meters tall and weighing 75 kilograms, the T800 delivers up to 450 Nm of joint torque, reaches speeds of 3 meters per second, and operates for up to four hours on a modular solid state battery. Equipped with 43 degrees of freedom, advanced perception sensors, and real time Al control, the T800 demonstrates complex actions including front kicks and martial arts style movements. This marks a shift from laboratory demos toward physically capable humanoid systems designed for logistics, service, and industrial collaboration. Why it matters: humanoid robotics is moving beyond mobility and into strength, balance, and controlled force. This is a key step toward machines that can safely and effectively operate in human environments.

    Most robotics simulation debates talk about realism. The real issue is iteration cost. The environment type is irrelevant. What forces repeated testing is spatial variability, not indoors or outdoors. In outdoor robotics every software change forces teams to revisit the same environment. Even in the most minimal case a two person field test costs about 520 euros per day in unavoidable labor alone. Avoiding only three repeated visits already saves 1,560 euros in a single location, before considering travel, hardware damage or delays. A metrically accurate digital twin does not replace real world validation. It replaces redundant validation caused by software iteration. After roughly four update cycles the environment pays for itself and every additional update becomes direct savings. Simulation fidelity is not a visual improvement. It is an operational cost control mechanism. #robotics #simulation #autonomoussystems #digitaltwin #robotdevelopment
