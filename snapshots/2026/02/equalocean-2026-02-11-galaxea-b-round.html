Galaxea AI (星海图) Secures 10 Billion RMB Series B Funding

Automotive Author: EqualOcean News, Leci Zhang, Yiran Xing Editor: Yiran Xing Feb 12, 2026 11:33 AM (GMT+8)
 X 

February 11 News – Embodied AI startup Galaxea AI (星海图) has recently completed a 1 billion yuan Series B financing round. To date, Galaxea AI’s cumulative financing has reached nearly 3 billion yuan, with its valuation expected to enter the "ten-billion-yuan" (decacorn) tier following this round.

Investors in this Series B round include industrial capital firms Jinding Capital (金鼎资本), BAIC Capital (北汽产投), and Bihong Investment (碧鸿投资), as well as institutions such as Loyal Valley Capital (正心谷资本), Qianhai Ark (前海方舟), and InnoVen Capital (毅峰资本). Existing shareholders, including Cathay Capital (凯辉基金), Meituan Dragonball (美团龙珠), Capital Today (今日资本), Xiang He Capital (襄禾资本), and GL Ventures (高瓴创投), continued to follow up with overallotments or full-quota investments.

According to reports, Galaxea AI has secured orders for several thousand units from developers and industrial clients. In 2026, the company plans to focus on expanding into the production application market, primarily targeting three scenarios: intelligent manufacturing, logistics, and commercial services.
