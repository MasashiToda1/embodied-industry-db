## Model Description

Thinker-WM is a unified multimodal world model that jointly models and generates **RGB, depth, and action** through a shared intermediate Transformer backbone with modality-specific input/output experts. It is pretrained on **20K hours of egocentric data** and **10K hours of robot data** (≈4K hours with aligned depth supervision), and ranks **1st** on three public benchmarks: **EWMBench**, **World Olympiad**, and **World Arena**.

This repository contains the official **inference release** of the model weights. For full documentation, installation, and usage, see the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/UBTECH-Robot/Thinker-WM).

## Supported Inference Modes

| mode | inputs | outputs |
|---|---|---|
| `wam` | prompt + first_frame + robot | video + actions (+depth) |
| `wm` | prompt + first_frame | video (+depth) |
| `vla` | prompt + first_frame + robot | actions |
| `a2v` | first_frame + actions + robot | video |
| `v2a` | video + robot | actions |
| `uncond` | first_frame + robot | video + actions |

For the WorldArena Track-1 evaluation, the reported EWMScore is the unweighted mean of 12 metrics covering visual quality, content consistency, physics adherence, 3D accuracy, and controllability. Dynamic Degree, Flow Score, and Motion Smoothness are excluded because of their sensitivity to frame rate and temporal sampling density.
