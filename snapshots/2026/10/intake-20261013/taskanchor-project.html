What “in-context learning” means here +

Earlier human interactions provide visual context that changes subsequent behavior of the same trained policy. This is task-conditioned adaptation within the episode, rather than evidence of learning arbitrary new skills.

Offline-learned parameters stay fixed during inference. TaskAnchor constructs temporary history-conditioned fast weights and discards them after the current forward pass. The model was jointly post-trained before evaluation.

Evidence, timestamps and limits of interpretation +

Event times follow the latest manuscript’s selected frames and were checked against the supplied videos. The 13-second restored-scene images in the ICL explainer are manually selected frames. No internal memory, coordinate predictions or scanner-state logs were supplied.

For counting, grasping difficulty alone does not establish a memory failure. For checkout, visible presentation at the scanner does not independently confirm scanner acceptance. External-view matching videos include privacy masks.

Scope and materials +

This page accompanies the supplied anonymous manuscript. Results use its reported values and protocols; demonstrations illustrate behavior without replacing aggregate evaluation. Method animations are explanatory schematics; the coordinate intervention shows recorded rollouts.

Completely unseen tasks, object configurations and execution structures have not been extensively evaluated. The supplied materials do not contain raw inference-time memory weights, predicted-coordinate traces or a live model service.
