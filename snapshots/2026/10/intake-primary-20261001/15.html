Price goal $15,000, available to pre-order for a refundable $499 - 1.2m (3'11"), 35kg (77 lb), 25+2 DOF - Ships this summer with the full BOM, CAD files, sim environment, and a pre-trained walking policy, all on GitHub - 50–100 hours to assemble

The Humanoid Hub

@TheHumanoidHub

Humanoids are getting their first true kit. @asimovinc V1 is a 1.2 meter, 35 kilogram biped with 25 actuated joints, full mechanical and simulation files on GitHub, and a DIY kit targeting a 15,000 dollar build cost. Most fleets are locked inside proprietary programs; Asimov is handing the hardware to anyone willing to build, train, and experiment.

Robotico

@roboticomarket

Initiatives like this are key to robot adoption at scale. Humanoid or not — the hardware that has the most user input is the one that will be best. That means inevitably, open source design will win. Just like what Meta did with the Open Compute Project.
