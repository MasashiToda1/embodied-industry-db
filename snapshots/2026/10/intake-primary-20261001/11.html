About

We are Simate.

We build embodied intelligence for everyday work.

Our research connects robot demonstrations, learning systems, and real-world evaluation.

WILDCARD

Build what doesn’t have a job title yet.

Some of the most important problems in embodied intelligence don’t fit neatly into an existing role.

We’re building foundation models and infrastructure for embodied intelligence. The direction is clear; many of the hardest problems are still open.

If you see a problem we should be solving—and can show why you’re the person to take it on—we want to hear from you.

01

You might be a fit if

  • You’ve built something technically difficult and can explain the decisions behind it.
  • You move comfortably between research, engineering, and real-world constraints.
  • You create momentum without waiting for a perfectly defined roadmap.
  • You care more about measurable progress than titles, ownership boundaries, or academic novelty alone.

Your strongest contribution may not match any role currently listed on our careers page.

02

Problems you could work on

Your work may span one or more of these areas:

  1. 01Foundation models for embodied intelligence
  2. 02Robot learning, control, and real-world deployment
  3. 03Data engines, simulation, evaluation, and feedback loops
  4. 04Training and inference infrastructure
  5. 05Developer tools and systems that accelerate the entire team

This is not a fixed job description. We’ll shape the role around the intersection of our highest-leverage problems and your strongest evidence of impact.

03

How we work

  1. 01

    Fast feedback from the real world

    New SOTA reaches our robots within a week. Your work goes directly into the team’s next decision.

  2. 02

    High ownership

    You define the problem, make the trade-offs, and drive the work through completion.

  3. 03

    Force-multiplying collaboration

    We value people who make the team sharper—not just those who produce strong individual output.

  4. 04

    End-to-end execution

    Reason from first principles, do what the problem requires, and deliver results that hold up outside the lab.

04

What to send

Email us one piece of work that best demonstrates your judgment and follow-through. It could be:

  • a paper
  • a codebase
  • a system you built
  • a technical case study
  • a failed attempt with a valuable lesson

Include brief answers to three questions:

  1. What did you build, and what was your specific contribution?
  2. What problem do you want to work on next?
  3. Why is Simate the right place to pursue it?

A résumé is optional. Evidence of work is required.

05

What happens next

  1. 01

    We review your work for technical depth, judgment, and relevance.

  2. 02

    If there is a promising match, you’ll speak directly with someone close to the problem.

  3. 03

    Together, we define a concrete problem space and determine whether it should become a role.

We aim to reply within 7 days.
