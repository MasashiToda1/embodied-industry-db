About Rerun

Rerun builds the unified data layer for Physical AI, from collection through training. Robotics, spatial computing, and embodied AI teams use Rerun to ingest multi-rate, multimodal data, query and curate it with dataframes or SQL, visualize it across the pipeline, and stream selected dataset mixtures into training and evaluation.

The open source Rerun SDK provides logging, storage, query, transformation, visualization, and training tools that developers can run locally and extend. Rerun Hub adds a commercial data catalog and storage engine for teams working with larger collections backed by object storage. Both use the same data model and APIs, so workflows can grow without replacing the tools used during early development.

Rerun Technologies AB is a remote-first Swedish company with founders based in Stockholm and teammates around the world. The company develops in the open, maintains the Rerun repository under the MIT license, and works with the robotics and Physical AI community through GitHub, Discord, documentation, examples, and direct product collaboration.
