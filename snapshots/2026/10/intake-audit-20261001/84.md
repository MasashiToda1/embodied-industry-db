# rb10_iros_double

Derived from [rainbowrobotics/rb10_iros_double_trim](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rainbowrobotics/rb10_iros_double_trim)
at revision `f7e8b828374180581532ec75112c0eb6ffcda91d`.

Only `observation.images.front` is pixel-rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise.
Its dimensions are now width 640 × height 480. AV1 is retained (SVT-AV1,
CRF 30, preset 12, GOP 2); rotating pixels requires lossy re-encoding.
Wrist videos and numeric data files are byte-identical to the source.
All 197 episodes, 171789 retained frames, timestamps and episode boundaries
are preserved. Front-camera global and episode image statistics are recomputed.
The source's logical-trim video offsets are preserved, including unused
frames physically stored outside the selected episode windows.
