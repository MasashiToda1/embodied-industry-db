    Robotis, a robot company, will introduce a customized cooperative robot "OM-Y" with physical artificial intelligence (AI) technology in June.

    According to Robotis on the 26th, OM-Y is an open-source robot manipulator (a device with similar functions to a human arm) that provides a module so that users can freely adjust joints and links as needed.

    The 'AI Manipulator', which will be released along with the launch of OM-Y, includes leaders, followers, and cameras, which are key elements of cooperative robots, and all are provided as open sources. In addition, the product is completely compatible with dynamic cells, so it can be used in a wide range of industries.

    Recently, "imitation learning" based on visual, language, and behavior (VLA) models has emerged as a major trend in the robot industry, and Robotis also plans to quickly release follow-up products in line with this trend.

    Robotis is also planning to expand support for the "reinforcement learning" platform in the simulator environment provided by Nvidia, Google DeepMind, and Open Robotics.
