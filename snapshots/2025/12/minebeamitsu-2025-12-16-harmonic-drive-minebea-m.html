MINEBEA MITSUMI Inc.

MinebeaMitsumi to Make Debut at CES 2026

-- Unveiling Humanoid Robotics Solutions, Featuring the High-Torque Micro Actuator Co-Developed with Harmonic Drive Systems --

MinebeaMitsumi Inc. (Head office: Nagano Prefecture; Representative Director, Chairman CEO: Yoshihisa Kainuma; hereinafter "MinebeaMitsumi") is pleased to announce its first participation as an exhibitor at CES 2026, the world's premier technology trade fair. The event will take place from Tuesday, January 6 to Friday, January 9, 2026.
Driven by the concept of "INTEGRATION" x "Collaboration," MinebeaMitsumi aims to promote its cutting-edge precision components to the global market at this event. Among the display will be the high-torque micro actuator for robot hands, jointly developed with Harmonic Drive Systems Inc. (hereinafter "Harmonic Drive Systems"). These components are critical for driving emerging technological innovation aimed at solving social challenges.

CES serves as a global hub for innovators, showcasing groundbreaking technologies and solutions for addressing social issues. For this event, MinebeaMitsumi will bring its future-oriented solutions like precision components and technologies that target high-growth sectors, such as humanoid robots, fully autonomous driving, and drones, which the company identifies as key growth drivers. "Five Pillars*".

Targeting the humanoid robots sector, MinebeaMitsumi will exhibit its various products engineered for durability, reliability, torque, sensitivity and quietness—all necessary for robot hands to perform work equivalent to that of a human. The booth will highlight the first public demonstration of the robot hand employing geared micro actuators, (W: 13 mm × H: 19.4 mm × L: 60.4 mm) co-developed with Harmonic Drive Systems and its related components. They provide both speed and power. As the issue with robot currently lies in robustness of the body, MinebeaMitsumi believes developing functions that enable the robot hand to perform human-like motions, such as holding heavy objects and executing detailed movements, is becoming increasingly important. One finger of the robot hand is capable of lifting an object weighing 5 kg. Moreover, the hand, which incorporates MinebeaMitsumi's strain sensor in the fingertip, can sense the hardness and softness of objects, allowing it to utilize different gripping motions with the same hand.

At this event, MinebeaMitsumi will showcase its strength as an "INTEGRATED Precision Component Manufacturer"—pursuing synergy effects by fusing its core product lineup, the "8 Spears," which are centered around ultra-precision machining and mass production technologies, and aggressively promote this integrated product lineup to engineers and partners globally, starting with the U.S. market.
Going forward, MinebeaMitsumi remains dedicated to driving global shifts toward electrification, energy-saving, and high-functionality across a wide range of areas including industrial, automotive, and IoT, in order to help building a sustainable society.

* MinebeaMitsumi's "Five Pillars" from Now on (Growth Drivers)

1) Product for AI server: Fan motor, HDD spindle motor, pressure sensor, optical connector, etc.
2) Product for humanoid robot: Bearing, ball screw, semiconductor, frameless motor, etc.
3) Product for fully autonomous driving (LiDAR): Cartridge bearing, BLDC motor, coil, power supply inductor, etc.
4) Product for drones: Bearing, brushless motor, GNSS antenna, optical marker, etc.
5) INTEGRATED product: Wing Handle (automotive application), ultra-small stepping motors for drone camera auto focus, etc.

About MinebeaMitsumi

MinebeaMitsumi is an INTEGRATED* precision components manufacturer that develops, manufactures, and sells a wide range of mechanical and electronic components, leveraging its core technologies in ultra-precision machining. The company holds the world's No.1 market share** in miniature & small-diameter ball bearings, which ensures smooth mechanical rotation, as well as single-cell protection ICs for lithium ion batteries. Its advanced technologies also extend to key components such as semiconductors, motors, and sensors.
MinebeaMitsumi's ultra-precision technologies are utilized across a broad applications—from household appliances such as air conditioners, vacuum cleaners, and hair dryers, to IT devices like gaming consoles, smartphones, and PCs; as well as in automobiles, aerospace and aviation products, and medical equipment. While often unseen, MinebeaMitsumi's components play a crucial role in supporting everyday life behind the scenes.
