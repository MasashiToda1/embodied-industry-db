TRI: pretrained large behavior models accelerate robot learning

1 Minute Read

"TRI trained a series of diffusion-based LBMs on almost 1,700 hours of robot data and conducted 1,800 real-world evaluation rollouts and over 47,000 simulation rollouts to rigorously study their capabilities. It found that LBMs:

  • Deliver consistent performance improvements relative to from-scratch policies
  • Enable new tasks to be learned with 3-5× less data in challenging settings requiring robustness to a variety of environmental factors
  • Improve steadily as pretraining data increases"
