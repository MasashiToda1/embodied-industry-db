Strategic collaboration with SAP and NVIDIA

  • 28. May 2025
GO BACK

At NEURA Robotics, we envision a future where cognitive robots take a central role in all fields — from private households to the business world. And we know: to truly lead on a global scale, technology must move from simulation to real-world action.

That’s why we are proud to announce our strategic collaboration with SAP and NVIDIA, combining our cutting-edge robotics with SAP’s business AI and NVIDIA’s digital twin technology — to bring physical Business AI to life.

In an increasingly autonomous world, robots need flexible, real-time access to business data: from process steps and pricing to manufacturing schedules and logistics. Only then can they move beyond one-off tasks and deliver exponential productivity gains. Together, we’re making this a reality — through the Neuraverse ecosystem, we provide the platform that brings both SAP Joule’s agents and NVIDIA’s AI models into real-world execution.

The result: intelligent agents that not only understand and decide — but also act: with NEURA’s robots as the interface to the real world.

Closing the gap: from digital insight to physical execution 

Traditional AI agents stop in the digital world — leaving the physical execution behind. But NEURA robots, powered by embedded SAP Joule’s agents, go further. Our joint solution addresses the core limitations of conventional systems:

  • No More Static AI: Our robots learn dynamically from enterprise data, business context, simulations, and real-world feedback.
  • Beyond Digital Insights: Robots act on insights immediately, closing the gap between decision and execution.
  • True Autonomy: By deploying cognitive AI to the edge — onto NEURA robots — tasks like adaptive manufacturing, inventory replenishment, or predictive maintenance become fully autonomous.

Our joint solution: real intelligence in motion 

Together with SAP and NVIDIA, we’ve built a system where SAP Joule’s agents are deployed directly into NEURA’s products, enabling:

  1. Edge-Powered Autonomy: Our robots make real-time decisions and act on them, from visual inspections to repairs.
  2. Task Orchestration: SAP Joule AI guides our robots through complex, multi-step workflows.
  3. Continuous Learning: Robots learn from:
  • SAP’s enterprise data and business processes
  • The SAP Business Data Cloud
  • NVIDIA Omniverse-based simulations
  • Real-world interactions and feedback
  1. Closed-Loop Execution: Every action streams data back into SAP and NEURA systems — for ongoing improvement and optimization.

How It Works — From Simulation to Reality 

  1. A new robotic use case is trained in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, using SAP application data and AI models.
  2. The scenario is validated in NVIDIA Omniverse using SAP’s business context and digital twins.
  3. Once validated, AI models and SAP agents are deployed to the Neuraverse ecosystem, or directly into the robot.
  4. During task execution, our robots send feedback data back to SAP and NVIDIA, closing the learning loop.

Our Vision in Action 

David Reger, CEO of NEURA Robotics: “We are proud to bring the power of our cognitive robots into the partnership with SAP and to jointly take the next step towards automating real-world tasks. Together, we are redefining what is possible and creating solutions that truly serve humans and transform industries — which fits perfectly with our ambition at NEURA.”

Empowering the Physical World with Cognitive AI

NEURA Robotics isn’t just building robots. We’re building a future where intelligent machines work alongside humans — making decisions, taking action, and constantly evolving. With SAP and NVIDIA by our side, this future is not years away — it starts today
