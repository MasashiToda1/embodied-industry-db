Company Leadership Update

Today we are announcing a significant transition within our leadership team. After much consideration, Geordie Rose is leaving his position as CEO of Sanctuary AI.

The Board and the executive team are fully committed to managing a transition and to positioning the company so we can continue the great work we’ve set out to do together. The Board has selected James Wells, as interim CEO with full support from the executive team.

Geordie is a pioneering force and visionary in the development of human-like intelligence and humanoid robotics, a category he created. His leadership has established Sanctuary as a leader in this emerging field. His commitment to advancing our mission and this category has inspired our progress and positioned us at the forefront of innovation. We are grateful for his contributions which have laid a strong foundation for the exciting future ahead.
