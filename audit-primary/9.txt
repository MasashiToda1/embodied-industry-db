Manual collection in closed labs is slow, expensive, and cannot produce the coverage real-world generalization needs.\nAxis is the\nCompounding Data Engine Accelerating Physical AI\n, and it unifies the two most scalable data sources there are —\nhigh-fidelity simulation\nand\nego-centric human data\n. Combining both pipelines gives the broadest environmental coverage available, compounding data diversity at foundation-model scale: every contribution improves the next policy, and each improved policy decides what data to collect next. Data stops being a one-time cost and starts compounding.\nScaling Physical AI for the Real World — Contributed by Everyone.\nHow It Works\nAnyone with a browser can contribute — and every task on the platform moves through one lifecycle:\nPre-training → Training → Post-training\n.\nPre-training: teach from scratch.\nYou teleoperate a simulated robot arm to complete manipulation tasks — pick up a mug, sort objects, open a drawer. Each session produces a\ntrajectory\n: a complete, timestamped recording of robot joint states, object poses, control actions, and task metadata. These demonstrations train a base policy for the task.\nPost-training: correct the robot.\nOnce a policy is trained, the task reopens in a new form. The policy attempts the task on its own while you watch; the moment it slips, you take over, fix the mistake, and hand control back. These corrections capture exactly what the policy cannot yet do — the highest-value data in robot learning — and train the next iteration of the policy.\nBehind the scenes, every trajectory is verified by replay in simulation, cleaned, and expanded in an IsaacSim-based augmentation backend into thousands of photorealistic, domain-randomized training samples. These samples train the models and imitation learning policies that generalize across tasks, environments, and robot embodiments.\nData no longer flows one way. A trained policy's weaknesses determine which tasks open next; the corrections contributors provide train the next policy; and the improved policy dictates what new data to collect. One platform, one community — a continuously improving policy.\nEvery signed trajectory is assigned a unique Data ID and recorded on\nBase\n, and alongside it go the task it came from and the
